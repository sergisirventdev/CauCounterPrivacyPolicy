Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CauCounter, developed by Sergi Sirvent, handles information when you use the mobile application.

Information We Collect

CauCounter does not require users to create an account and does not run its own backend server for user profiles.

The app may store game settings, team names, player names, match data, statistics, and imported match information locally on your device. This data is used to provide the app's scoring, statistics, import, and export features.

Camera and Gallery Access

The app may request camera access only when you choose to scan a QR code for importing match information. QR code contents are processed for the import feature.

The app may request photo or gallery access when you choose to save a generated statistics image to your device. This access is used only for that user-requested save action.

Advertising

CauCounter displays ads in the free version using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, app interaction data, and diagnostic information to provide, measure, and improve ads. Google may use this information according to its own policies.

You can learn more about Google's advertising practices at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

CauCounter offers an optional premium purchase to remove ads. Purchases are processed by Google Play. CauCounter uses RevenueCat to manage and validate purchase status, such as whether the ad-free entitlement is active.

RevenueCat may process purchase identifiers, product identifiers, app user identifiers generated by the purchase SDK, transaction status, and related diagnostic information. You can read RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate the app, save your local preferences and statistics, import and export match data when requested, display ads in the free version, validate purchases, remove ads for premium users, and diagnose technical issues.

Sharing

CauCounter does not sell personal or sensitive user data. Data may be processed by service providers used by the app, including Google AdMob, Google Play, and RevenueCat, for the purposes described above. Data may also be disclosed if required by law.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it from within the app where available, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Purchase records are managed by Google Play and RevenueCat according to their retention policies.

Children

CauCounter is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact the developer so the issue can be reviewed.

Security

CauCounter uses platform and service provider mechanisms to protect data during purchase validation and ad delivery. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but reasonable safeguards are used.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the contact email listed for CauCounter on Google Play.
